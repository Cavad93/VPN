Installation Successful

CavadVPN has been installed on your Mac.

Getting Started

Open Terminal and run:

cavadvpn --help

To connect to your VPN server:

cavadvpn connect --server YOUR_SERVER_IP:443

Configuration is stored at ~/.config/cavadvpn/config.yaml.

Uninstall

To remove CavadVPN, delete /Applications/CavadVPN and run:

sudo rm /usr/local/bin/cavadvpn
